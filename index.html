Use the physical Power key to start when off. Hold for two seconds to force power off. Caelestia session options offer Lock, Log Out, Restart and Power Off. Startup runs Limine and CachyOS before the SDDM Locklike welcome. Press a key or tap the welcome to open sign in; the first key is not a password character. Type your password and press Enter. Closing the lid locks the existing Hyprland session.

Drag to orbit; wheel or pinch to zoom. Enter or Space focuses the laptop. A new inward gesture shows the complete desktop; outward gestures return one view. Escape closes a desktop surface before returning one view. The left Caelestia bar contains the launcher, five workspaces, clock, network, battery and session controls. Select the clock for Dashboard; the top-right bell opens notifications and the bottom-right sliders open Utilities. Tab to matching native controls. Alt+D opens Launcher; Alt+1 through Alt+5 changes workspace; add Shift to move the active window. Wi-Fi, volume, brightness, weather and resource values belong to this local simulation. Tap the front notch to open or close the lid; tap the camera cover to move it.
