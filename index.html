Drag · Zoom · Select screen

Drag with a mouse or one finger to rotate the view. Use the mouse wheel or pinch with two fingers to zoom. Zoom in to center the laptop and keyboard, then use a new zoom gesture to view the entire desktop. The desktop keeps its proportions with dark margins where needed; its taskbar and all screen edges remain visible. Each zoom gesture can change only one stage; pause scrolling or lift your fingers before changing another stage. Zoom out or press Escape to return one stage at a time. Use the arrow keys to rotate, plus and minus to zoom, and Enter or Space to focus on the laptop. Release a zoom key before changing another stage. Click or tap the notch at the front center of the laptop to close or open the lid, or use the Laptop lid button with the keyboard. Closing the lid returns to the scene; the screen and camera cover are available again when the lid is fully open. Click or tap the camera cover above the display to open or close it, or use the Camera privacy cover button with the keyboard. The laptop desktop is currently empty.
